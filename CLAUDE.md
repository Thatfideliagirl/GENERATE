Read this first. It is everything you need to continue.

## What the product is
Generate is a document maker for freelancers and businesses in Nigeria and beyond: invoices, receipts and contracts that carry the user's name, logo and signature. Brand name is Generate (logo wordmark is written GENErate). The logo files are in public/ (logo.png, favicon.png, icon-192.png). "Vellum" was an old working name and must not appear anywhere. If you see it, replace it. A fallback localStorage key `folio.v1` / `vellum.v1` exists in src/data/repo.ts only to read old data.

## Stack
Vite 5, React 18, TypeScript strict (noUnusedLocals and noUnusedParameters on), react-router-dom v6 HashRouter, jspdf and html2canvas (dynamic import) for PDF. No UI library. Plain CSS in src/styles (global.css, paper.css, landing.css).

Commands: npm install, npm run dev, npm run build (runs tsc then vite build), npm run preview.

## Structure
src/pages: Landing, Setup, Dashboard, Editor (NewDoc and OpenDoc), ContractTemplates, Profile.
src/components: DocumentPaper (the document itself, used for preview and PDF), PaperPreview (scales 760px paper to the screen), LookControls (layout, font, colours, paper), SignatureModals (draw and type), ReminderModal, ProfileForm, Modal, Fields, TopBar, Toast.
src/lib: types, constants (BRAND, currencies, trades, fonts, accents), calc (totals, part payments, status), factory, templates (3 contract templates), whatsapp (links and reminder messages), pdf, image, format, style, text.
src/data: repo.ts (Repo interface with localStorage implementation) and store.tsx (React context).

## Key decisions
Data goes through the Repo interface (async load and save). To add Supabase, write a supabaseRepo with the same two functions and change the single line `export const repo = localRepo` in src/data/repo.ts.
Status is computed, never stored by hand: paid, part, overdue, sent, draft, signed. Part payments live in doc.payments.
Currency is per document. Dashboard totals are grouped per currency.
Paper is always 760px wide and scaled down for small screens. The same component renders preview and PDF.
Look system: layouts classic, modern, minimal, boxed, split, bold. Fonts editorial, serif, sans, elegant, rounded, mono. Accent can be a named colour or any hex. Text colour is free. Paper is white by default or ivory.
Contracts use {{business}}, {{client}}, {{owner}}, {{date}}, {{fee}}, {{start}}, {{end}} placeholders and lines starting with "# " as headings. They are starter templates, not legal advice. Keep a note saying a lawyer should check them.
WhatsApp: numbers starting with 0 get 234 added. Reminder tones: friendly, firm, final.

## Brand look
The whole product now uses one look: powder blue #E6EDF3 background, white cards, navy ink #13203A, one clay accent #B4603F, Geist for text, Geist Mono for small labels, and Instrument Serif italic for the single human word in a heading (wrap it in <em>). The logo keeps its own green and gold. Light is always the default, whatever the phone or laptop is set to. Dark (deep navy) is only used when the person picks it (ThemeToggle in the side bar, the phone header, or Settings; lib/theme.ts stores generate.theme and sets data-theme on html). People can also pick an accent colour in Settings (clay, emerald, ocean, plum, gold, rose; generate.accent, data-accent and --accent-base replace --clay inside the app only, and in light mode also --em, so the main buttons, New and chosen tabs take the colour). A first time tour of Home (components/Tour.tsx, steps in Dashboard.tsx, targets marked with data-tour) runs once per account per device and can be restarted from Settings. Coco disliked the mono font: Geist Mono is gone, --mono now points at Geist, and section labels on the site are clay sentence case with a short line before them. Avoid generic gradients, purple, and rows of identical icon cards. Default document look for new profiles is classic, editorial font, navy accent, charcoal text; documents are the user's own brand, so they can pick any look.

## Inside the app (rebuilt in the new look)
src/components/AppShell.tsx wraps every signed in page (App.tsx Private does it). Laptop (1000px and up): side bar with logo and a fold button (folds to icons, remembered as generate.side), a New button (MakeSheet), the business card, Home, All documents, Your work (Invoices, Receipts, Contracts, Signed documents, Customers), Profile and settings, Owner page for admins, then a Dark mode or Light mode switch and a "Generate homepage, Pricing, help and news" card that links to the public site (Coco did not want "Back to website"). Phone: a bottom bar (Home, Documents, raised New, Customers, Profile) on the list pages; the phone header has the theme switch and the profile avatar. Business initials without a logo show as a navy rounded square (BrandMark .mono), not a circle. Icons in components/Icons.tsx; ThemeToggle.tsx; styles in global.css and app.css (a- classes).
What each page is for (agreed with Coco): Home is money and activity at a glance, driven by one time filter (Today, This week, This month, This year, All time; lib/period.ts, components/PeriodTabs.tsx): money received, waiting to be paid, overdue, part paid; a chart of money received per month (6 months, or 12 for this year and all time); counts that open each section (invoices made, receipts issued, contracts, documents signed, customers); then Who owes you (WhatsApp Remind) and Recent documents. A New button (header on phones and tablets, side bar on laptops) opens the make sheet. Each kind of document has its own page with its own summary and list: /app/invoices, /app/receipts, /app/contracts (pages/Sections.tsx DocSection) and /app/signed (SignedDocs). All documents (/app/documents) shows how many of each kind were made in the chosen time (cards that open the section pages) and every document grouped by day. Rows use components/DocRow.tsx with a quick PDF download.
Signed documents: when a PDF is signed and downloaded, a copy is kept on that device in IndexedDB (lib/signedStore.ts, components/useSigned.ts) so it shows under Signed documents with download and remove. They are not uploaded and do not move between devices yet; Supabase Storage would be the next step.
Customers (pages/Customers.tsx, /app/customers and /app/customers/:id; lib/customers.ts; components/CustomerSheets.tsx): add, edit, remove, import from phone contacts (Contact Picker, Android Chrome only), a CSV or text file, or a pasted list (one per line, any order of name, phone, email). A customer page shows what they owe, total billed, their documents, and New invoice, New receipt and WhatsApp buttons. Documents link to a customer by doc.customerId or by the same name. In the editor, Choose from customers fills the client; after Save or Preview, a new client name triggers "Add them to your customers?" once per name (the question travels in router state across the save redirect). /app/new/invoice?customer=id starts a document for that customer. Stored in AppData.customers; Supabase table public.customers (supabase/customers.sql, also at the end of schema.sql). Until Coco runs that SQL, supabaseRepo keeps customers on the device and everything else keeps working.
Profile (/app/profile) is a view of the business with pen buttons that open /app/profile/edit (the ProfileForm with Save and Cancel), and Settings: Appearance light or dark, backup and restore, sign out, Privacy and Go to website.
Editor: status tag in the header, white cards, live preview beside the form from 1180px, look controls folded away. Contract templates show filled in previews. Sign in and sign up (AuthShell, .auth2 in auth.css) are one centred white card on powder blue, no side panel: logo and Back to home at the top, a Sign in or Start free switch (Coco asked to remove the floating signature line behind the card) at the top of the card, and three small promises under it (Free to use, Made for your phone, Send on WhatsApp). The website keeps two buttons on purpose: Sign in for people with an account, Start free for new people. Owner and privacy pages share the look in auth.css, each with a Back pill.

## Accounts and owner page
Routes: /signup, /signin, /forgot, /reset, /privacy, /admin, /setup (needs an account), /app (needs account and profile), /app/sign (sign a document).
Two back ends sit behind the same interfaces. src/data/authService.ts and repoService.ts pick one: with VITE_SUPABASE_URL and VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY set (see .env.example, .env is gitignored) the app uses supabaseAuth.ts and supabaseRepo.ts, otherwise the on device demo (auth.ts localAuth, repo.ts localRepo, accounts in localStorage, demo banners shown). Never put the service_role key in the app.
Supabase auth: PKCE flow, email and password, sign up passes full_name, business_name, business_type, trade as user metadata for the database trigger. If email confirmation is on, signUp returns null, the typed business details are kept on the device (savePending) and saved on first sign in. The owner is whoever is in the app_admins table (public.is_admin() rpc), not an email in the code. ADMIN_EMAILS in lib/constants.ts is only for the demo (Coco's two emails). Owners land on /admin after sign up and sign in; on phones the owner page is a shield icon in the home header and a row in Settings. Password reset: sendReset emails a link back to /#/reset, where Reset in pages/Auth.tsx calls updatePassword. Needs Site URL and Redirect URLs set in Supabase, Authentication, URL Configuration. supabaseRepo saves only what changed since the last save, in order, and the store shows a Try again screen if loading fails so nothing is ever saved over data that did not load.
The Supabase side was tested with in memory stand ins for the client and a local Postgres for the SQL, not against a live project. First thing to do with real keys: sign up, make an invoice, reload, sign in on another browser, then sign up the owner account, run the app_admins insert, and open /admin.
The owner page is src/pages/Admin.tsx, an owner dashboard: signups total, today, last 7 days, this month; a 30 day signups chart; who is signing up by business type and trade; usage counts (documents, invoices, receipts, contracts, customers, made this week, businesses active this week) from the security definer function admin_activity in supabase/owner_stats.sql (counts only, answers owners only; lib/ownerStats.ts, which counts the browser's own data in demo mode); a note that visitors, countries and time spent come from Vercel Web Analytics once online; then the signups list with search, filters and CSV. It must never load documents. Row level security stops the owner reading documents even if the page tried. src/pages/Privacy.tsx is a draft that needs a lawyer.
Sign up is the full business setup (details, typed Something else options, logo, signature, payment, tax, social links, password). The default look is edited later on the Profile page. Social handles live in profile.socials (Instagram, Twitter, TikTok) and each document has a showSocials tick box that prints them at the bottom of the paper.
Sign up collects the WhatsApp number on purpose: it is the number the send on WhatsApp button uses (stored as profile.phone).

## Database (Supabase)
supabase/schema.sql builds everything: profiles (who signed up, owner can read), business_settings (private profile json and number counters), documents (id, user_id, type, number, status, data jsonb), saved_templates, app_admins (owner accounts, closed to the app), with row level security. A trigger fills profiles from the sign up metadata, so supabaseAuth.signUp must pass options.data with full_name, business_name, business_type, trade. supabaseRepo maps AppData.profile to business_settings.data, AppData.seq to business_settings.seq, AppData.docs to documents.data and AppData.templates to saved_templates. The schema was tested on a local Postgres with stand ins for Supabase: owner sees profiles only, users see only their own rows, signed out sees nothing, deleting a user removes their rows.

## Sign a document (built)
Route /app/sign, src/pages/SignDocument.tsx and src/lib/signpdf.ts. The person opens a PDF or picture (png, jpg, webp), pages are drawn with pdfjs-dist (legacy build, loaded only on this page), they add their saved or a newly drawn signature, initials, date or text, drag and resize on any page, and download a signed PDF written with pdf-lib. Everything is a picture placed by fraction of the page, so it works on rotated pages and cropped pages. All on the device, nothing uploaded. Not built: sending a document to someone else to sign (needs Supabase), several people signing, locking or certifying the file. Needs a decision on free or Generate more.

## Free signing and sign out
Anyone can sign a document free at /sign (SignDocument with isPublic, no account; the website has a "Sign any document, free" section after the questions, components/home/FreeSign.tsx, and a "Sign a PDF free" menu link). Before the first download an email is asked for (EmailGate) and saved with lib/leads.ts to public.sign_leads (supabase/leads.sql: anyone may insert, only the owner may read), then "Enjoy more with Generate" invites them to start free. The owner dashboard shows these emails (count, last 7 days, how many went on to sign up, latest list, CSV).
Sign out is on the laptop home header, at the bottom of the side menu, as an icon in the phone header, in Settings, and on the website header and phone menu when signed in. The website header has a menu button on phones (h-menu, h-mnav) with every link plus Start free and Sign in.
